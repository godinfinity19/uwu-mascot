# UwU — master character prompt

Paste the **Master prompt** at the start of any image/video prompt, then add the scene. Always attach `docs/reference/uwu_turnaround.webp` and `docs/renders/uwu_front_neutral.png` as character references when the tool allows it.

## Master prompt (full)

```
UwU, an original cute mascot creature, about 30 cm tall, chibi proportions (head about 60% of total height).

HEAD: a large soft cream (#f9e7d4) onion-bulb-shaped head: narrow at the top where it flows seamlessly into the base of the curl, widest at the full round cheeks just below eye level, with a rounded lower rim that overhangs the small body. The head is about twice as wide as the torso. Pink (#f2abb3) blush along the outer edges of the head.

BIG CURL (signature feature): one large curl rising from the top of the head, peach (#fbd2bf) at the stem, turning saturated pink (#e58fab / #e99fbf) along its arch, ending in a round purple (#a46a9a) ball wrapped by a spiral groove. The curl always leans toward the character's own right (viewer's left in front view).

TWO TUFTS: (1) a small flame-shaped crown tuft, peach with pink outside, just behind the stem of the curl on the viewer's right; (2) ONE floppy side tuft on the character's left side only (viewer's right): cream at the base, rising then curling its tip downward, lavender (#c47fae) underside.

EYES: two big glossy eyes set wide apart, dark plum-purple irises (#4d1634) in three tones: darkest under the upper lid, medium plum in the middle, and a light berry-pink band (#b2446f) along the bottom. Irises shifted slightly toward the nose. One white catchlight in the upper iris, a faint pink dot in the lower band, faint pupil. Thick upper eyelid, heaviest at the outer corner, with two short lashes at the outer corner. Cream-white sclera.

CHEEKS: soft salmon-pink blush ovals (#f7a1a2) under and outside each eye. A small filled golden-orange four-point star (#e69e58) sits on the character's LEFT cheek only (viewer's right), over the blush. Never on the other cheek, never missing.

MOUTH: small, simple, a gentle smile by default.

RUFF: a collar of almond-shaped feathers just under the head: in front view, two on each side: a long purple (#a46a9a) back feather angled down and outward, and a pink-rose (#e99fbf) front feather hanging over the chest; the middle of the chest stays cream.

BODY & LIMBS: small rounded cream torso. Two short stubby mitten-like arms, pink with lavender undersides. Two tiny pink feet with two toe lines each. A small fluffy pink (#e58fab) tail with lavender shading, peeking out under the arm on the character's left side.

PERSONALITY: sweet, curious, helpful, a little shy, very expressive; reads instantly at small sizes.

DO NOT: add a nose, ears, hands with fingers, clothes, hair other than the curl and two tufts, extra stars, or change colors; do not mirror the curl, tuft or cheek star to the other side; no humanoid proportions.
```

## Style add-ons (pick one)

**2D brand style (original artwork):**
```
Style: hand-drawn children's-book illustration, dark plum ink outline (#52263b) with a lighter second strand, soft watercolor washes with crayon grain, dry purple-grey ground shadow, warm cream paper background (#f9f1e6).
```

**3D CGI (ads, film):**
```
Style: feature-film 3D animated character, Pixar / Illumination quality, soft subsurface-scattered skin, finely groomed short fur on the ruff, tuft and tail, glossy wet eyes with real reflections, physically based materials, cinematic lighting.
```

**Flat / icon (UI, stickers):**
```
Style: clean flat vector illustration, simple shapes, solid fills with one soft shade per color, thin plum outline, no texture, transparent background.
```

## Short version (when the prompt has a tight character limit)

```
UwU mascot: cute chibi cream onion-shaped head with a big pink spiral curl ending in a purple ball (leaning to its right), small flame crown tuft, one floppy pink side tuft on its left, big glossy plum-purple eyes, salmon blush, a small golden four-point star on its left cheek only, purple-and-pink feather ruff, stubby pink arms, tiny pink feet, small pink tail. No nose, no ears.
```

## Negative prompt

```
nose, ears, fingers, human body, clothing, hair, extra tufts, star on both cheeks, missing cheek star, mirrored curl, realistic animal, scary, sharp teeth, text, watermark, logo
```

## Expressions (append when needed)

| Expression | Add |
|---|---|
| neutral | `calm, eyes open, small smile` |
| happy | `eyes closed as upward arcs (^ ^), big open smile, stronger blush, curl perked up` |
| uwu | `eyes closed as soft U-shaped arcs, mouth shaped like a small "w", strong blush, head tilted` |
| surprised | `eyes wide with small irises and extra shine, small round "o" mouth, curl springing straight up` |
| sad | `eyes slightly lowered with a teary shine, inner brows raised, small frown, curl and side tuft drooping` |
| shy | `eyes glancing down to the side, wavy little mouth, very strong blush, arms pulled to the chest` |
